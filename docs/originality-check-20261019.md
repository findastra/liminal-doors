# Liminal Doors: where the 37 floor ideas came from (originality check, 2026-10-19)

Astra asked: "run our copyright tool over these ideas to see if they were taken from somewhere else."
Unity Unicorn's copyright tools (`copyright-logger` and `copyright-catcher`, unity-unicorn PR #1) log the license of
asset folders inside a Unity project. They can't judge ideas, so this check was done by hand by Claude Opus 5.5.

**Decision (Astra, 2026-10-19): "just get rid of the worlds you found are copies, except mine of course."**
- Removed from the collection: floors 34 (Bubble Chair Cloud Lounge), 35 (Midnight Glass Garden, the glass wolf) and
  37 (Secret Undersea Galaxy).
- Also removed: plate 7's glass wolf and bubble chair reference panels.
- Dropped from the idea list: the exact Backrooms replica, and copying Copperhead's ASMR tools.
- Floor numbers stay as they were, so the collection now has 34 floors and skips 34, 35 and 37.
- Kept: Astra's own work, the Infinite Pole booth (01) and staircase (17).
- The glass wolf can come back only as the original asset with its creator's terms, or as our own model. See
  "Getting the real wolf" below.

**Method.**
1. Read Codex's idea inbox and floor register (2026-10-15). They list 16 VRChat worlds Astra gave as references and
   what each was for.
2. Looked each world up with VRChat's public world API (name, creator, date, description).
3. Searched the web for the closest well-known look-alikes and for the name.
4. Compared each of the 37 floors with what turned up.

**What this is not.** It is not legal advice, and it is not a reverse-image search of all 37 pictures. Ideas, moods
and game mechanics are generally free to reuse. The real risk is copying a specific creator's models, textures,
layouts, music or characters, or a world that is itself a copy of a film or show.

## The whole idea: doors to other places

- **The name.** No world, game or app called "Liminal Doors" turned up. The nearest are Roblox's horror game
  *DOORS* (numbered hotel doors) and an itch.io game called *Liminal VR*. Both are different enough.
- **Astral Bounds is the closest existing VRChat world.** It also has teleporting doors and hidden collectibles that
  pay off. Mechanics aren't owned, but keep the differences strong:
  - forward doors go to random places and keep a way back;
  - the places feel like real, ordinary empty spots;
  - you start in the pink phone booth;
  - the collectibles are glass animals, not crystals;
  - drop the underwater galaxy (Astra already has).
- **Suzume.** Lone free-standing doors in empty landscapes are the signature image of *Suzume* (a door standing in
  water among ruins). Floors 02, 24, 30 and 32 show a lone door in open land. The trope is old (Narnia, Monsters,
  Inc.), so it is fine. Just don't copy the film's white weathered door, its ruins, or the "door into the Ever-After"
  framing.

## Floor by floor

**Your own work (fine):**
- 01 Pinkscape: the booth you spawn in, from Astra's Infinite Pole, now a handmade K6 kiosk in this repo.
- 17 Soft Staircase: the planned spiral is Infinite Pole's own staircase.

**Copied from a specific creator. Don't build these without the source or permission:**
- **35 Midnight Glass Garden, the glass wolf.** The brief asks for "the actual prismatic material reference" from
  Crystals Absence. See "Getting the real wolf" below.
- **34 Bubble Chair Cloud Lounge.** The chair that molds around a seated avatar is also from Crystals Absence.
  - Asset lead (not confirmed as the one Arkîe used): DynamicMochiMochiShader by Nine Gates / OmochiNobiru,
    free, MIT license (keep its license notice). https://booth.pm/en/items/3129610
  - The hanging acrylic "bubble chair" shape is also Eero Aarnio's famous 1968 design. A round hanging chair is
    fine; an exact replica of his product isn't needed.
- **37 Secret Undersea Galaxy.** Taken from Astral Bounds, and Astra no longer wants it. **The floor needs a new
  idea.** Three starters that keep the Nautilus:
  - **Shell Spiral Library:** shelves that wind inward through ever-smaller chambers, like a nautilus shell.
  - **Lighthouse Lens Room:** sitting inside a giant Fresnel lens at night.
  - **Tide Clock Hall:** a dry hall where the tide marks the hours on the walls.
- **Backrooms "exact replica"** (a candidate outside the 37). Kane Pixels' Backrooms films and *Backrooms: The Anime*
  are copyrighted works; the yellow-room meme itself is shared. Build a Backrooms-*like* level, not a copy of one
  creator's layout.
- **"Every ASMR tool" from Copperhead ASMR Barbershop.** Recreating each tool's look and scripts would copy that
  world. Use store assets with licenses that allow it, or make our own.

**References that are themselves fan copies of films or shows. Keep our version clearly our own:**
- 02 Faraway Field: its grass-and-sky reference recreates the pink realm inside Lion's mane from *Steven Universe*.
  A long-grass sunset field is generic. Avoid an all-pink field with a lone tree.
- 03 Pool After Closing: the Poolrooms look (a Backrooms setting). Generic tiled pools are fine.

**From real places or real things (fine, credit kindly):**

  gently, and say where the idea comes from.

## Getting the real wolf (floor 35)

Astra wants the wolf to be "the asset that the original creator used," and big.

- **What's known.** The wolf is in **Crystals Absence by Arkîe** (wrld_68f4641c-67c8-4ccc-8eda-98e415c345bd,
  5,279 favorites).
  - The world's description is just its name.
  - VRChat only shows Arkîe's profile when you're signed in.
  - Web searches found no store listing for the wolf.
  - So the mesh and shader source are still unknown.
- **What can't be done.** Pulling the wolf out of the uploaded world (ripping). It breaks VRChat's Terms of Service
  and the creator's copyright, and it's the kind of copying this check exists to stop.
- **Ways to get it properly:**
  1. **Look in the world.** Astra opens Crystals Absence and checks for a credits board. She also checks Arkîe's
     VRChat profile bio for links (BOOTH, Gumroad, X, Discord).
  2. **Ask Arkîe.** Ask which wolf model and shader they used, and whether it's for sale or free. Many world makers
     use store assets and are happy to say which ones.
  3. **If it's a store asset,** buy or download it from the store and log it in Unity Unicorn's copyright-logger:
     source, artist, terms, and whether credit is needed. Scaling it up to a giant is easy once it's ours.
  4. **If it's Arkîe's own work,** ask permission, or make our own: any wolf model whose license allows world use,
     plus a faceted prismatic shader (dark facets, clear patches, rainbow edge flashes). One lead, not checked: the
     Gemstone Effect shader on BOOTH, https://booth.pm/en/items/8649170.
